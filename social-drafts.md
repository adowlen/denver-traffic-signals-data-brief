## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

0 of 1,288.

Denver's traffic signal register lists every signal in the city -
and records an installation date for none of them. Rebuild date:
none. Major retime date: none. All 1,288 records, on all three
fields. The register has also not been edited since May 24, 2024, so
the fleet's age cannot be read from the public record at all.

What the register can do is locate every signal, and joining it to
crash reports shows 34.5% of geocoded crashes since 2021 happened
within 150 feet of a signal - with KSI crashes at 2.8% of near-signal
crashes against 2.1% elsewhere, a gap this brief reports with its
exposure caveat attached, not as a cause.

Full brief, data, and methods:
